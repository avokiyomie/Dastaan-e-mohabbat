♡ A Letter for Ahmed

Ahmed,

If someone had told me that our story would begin on January 7, 2026, with something as ordinary as you asking me for my number, I probably would have laughed.

You told me to call you if my boss arrived. It was such a small request, but I never knew that giving you my number would become the beginning of something that would mean so much to me.

I grew to love the little things about you — the conversations, the laughter, and the moments that perhaps seemed ordinary to everyone else but somehow stayed with me. You became someone I remembered in quiet moments, someone I thought about when I prayed, someone I included in the softest corners of my hopes.

And maybe that is why the hurt has been so difficult to carry.

You didn't hurt me just once. You hurt me more than once, more than twice, and more times than I ever wanted to count. Every time, a part of me had to learn how to forgive something my heart never deserved in the first place.

And still, somehow, I stayed.
Not because I didn't know my worth. But because I loved you.

I kept hoping that the person I loved would eventually become the person I could trust completely. I kept believing that perhaps this time would be different. I kept making excuses for a heart that was already tired of making them.

But love should never require someone to repeatedly break their own heart just to keep a relationship alive.

If you ever choose to betray me again, I will leave.

I won't beg. I won't compete. I won't ask why. I won't keep returning to the same wound hoping it will stop hurting. I will simply disappear from your life and choose the peaceful life I deserve.

I will go quietly, with no hatred in my heart.

And perhaps one day, when the memories have become gentle instead of painful, I will look back at January 7 and remember that something as simple as a request for my number became the beginning of a story that taught me how deeply I could love — and how deeply I needed to love myself too.

Still, despite everything, I pray for you. I pray Allah protects you, guides you, gives you a heart that knows loyalty, and fills your life with the kind of peace that does not require hurting another person to find it.

And when I make my own prayers, I ask Allah: If you are meant to remain in my life, let there be honesty, loyalty, peace, and something worth keeping. And if you are not, give me the courage to let go without losing myself.

I don't want a love that constantly makes me wonder whether I am enough. I want a love that makes me feel safe — a love that keeps its promises when nobody is watching.

I want something peaceful. Something honest. Something that stays.

And if you cannot give me that, then I hope I have the strength to walk away and find my peace.

— with love, but also with a heart that has finally learned its worth. ♡

♡ Our Story

It all started on January 7, 2026.

You first asked for my number, telling me to call you if my boss arrived. I didn't know then that something so simple would become the beginning of our story.

Later, you texted me that you needed sugar for your coffee. Just a little message, but somehow it became one of those tiny memories I still remember.

Then, on another day, we met inside your car. You had already bought me a Del Monte pineapple drink, and somewhere in that moment, we shared our first kiss.

And then life went on.

More conversations, more memories, more laughter, more love, and eventually, more pain too.

But whenever I think about where it all began, I remember the little things first — the number you asked for, your coffee needing sugar, that pineapple drink, and our first kiss.

Maybe that's what makes our story ours.
It wasn't one grand beginning. It was a collection of little moments that slowly became us. ♡
