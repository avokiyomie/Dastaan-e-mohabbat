♡ A Letter for You

Ahmed,

If love were an old Pakistani story, I think ours would begin quietly — somewhere between the golden hush of evening, the fragrance of chai, and a sky turning purple over the rooftops.

It would not need grand words. It would live in the little things: in remembering you, in wondering how your day went, and in the quiet happiness of knowing that somewhere in this world, there is you.

“If I could leave one sentence in the margins of your life, it would be this: I am grateful that my heart found you.”

I hope you always know that you are someone I want to see happy, someone I want to see protected, and someone I quietly pray good things for.

So let this be our little tale — not hurried, not perfect, just tender. A love remembered in quiet duas, moonlit evenings, and the kind of affection that feels a little like coming home.

— always, with love ♡
